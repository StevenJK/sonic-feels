DAY·COUNT
Milestone alerts
A notification as an event you've flagged with the bell gets close — 100, 50, 30, 14, 7, 3, 2, 1 days out, and on the day itself. Android gives a web app no alarm clock, so these arrive when Chrome next wakes the app, which may be hours late and may not happen at all. Treat them as a nudge, never as the thing you're relying on.
ALLOW ALERTS
Icon badge
Puts the day count of the top event on the app icon, where the phone supports it. Nothing OS may or may not draw it.
SHOW BADGE
Backup
Everything lives in this browser's storage on this phone and nowhere else. Clearing site data deletes it for good, so keep a file somewhere.
About
This app makes no network requests at all. No account, no server, no analytics, no fonts or scripts fetched from anywhere. The only files it ever asks for are its own, and the worker keeps those on the device so it opens with the radio off. Your events are yours alone — they are never sent anywhere, because there is nowhere for them to be sent.
